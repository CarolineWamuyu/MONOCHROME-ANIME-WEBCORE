20 NOV 2026 mood: sleepy ☾

screentone is just math wearing a costume

Spent the weekend reverse-engineering old manga tones. A 60-line screen at 20% is just a grid of dots with a radius function. Built a little generator, then spent four hours just staring at moiré patterns.

read more

The best part: rotate two tone layers 15° against each other and you get rosettes, the same ones you see in old comic printing. I'm putting the generator into the Experiments section soon.

21 OCT 2026 mood: thoughtful ✎

why everything here is black & white

People ask if I'm "against colour". Not at all! But monochrome makes me work harder at contrast, rhythm and type. And clients remember the work that's brave enough to stay quiet.

read more

Also, black ink is cheap and the risograph only has one drum. Constraints all the way down.

30 SEP 2026 mood: hyped ★

my desk setup (2026 edition)

One old drawing tablet, one new monitor, a sticker-covered laptop and a jar of pens I never use because I draw everything digitally anyway. Photos coming once I tidy up (never).
